1. Why this toolbox?

Chapter 1: Why this toolbox?

The "Toolbox for City Changers" is for people who want to make their neighbourhood safer, greener, more inclusive, and more resilient to climate change, and need practical tools to do so.

It brings together experiences from Berlin, Vienna, and Ljubljana. Changing Cities from Berlin supports more than 70 initiatives that demand superblocks for their neighbourhoods – for a more liveable city. Wir machen Wien from Vienna grew out of Platz für Wien, the city's biggest-ever campaign for better public spaces, which gathered more than 57,000 signatures. And Prostorož is a Slovenian non-profit urban design studio leading the way in creating human-centred spaces.

This handbook is for everyone asking:

How can we make our city more liveable?

All the following chapters help answer this question step by step. We believe that cities should be designed for people and that everyone has the right to a beautiful and safe neighbourhood. If you do, too, keep reading.

How to use this handbook

If you are already part of an initiative, do not read everything in order. Start with the chapter that fits your next decision: defining a vision, planning a first action, building alliances, or documenting impact.

What this handbook offers

As an essential part of the Toolbox for City Changers, this handbook summarises:

How superblocks work as a planning concept to make your neighbourhood safer, greener, and more people-friendly (chapter 2)

How tactical urbanism can turn these ideas into reality with little effort and cost

How street design tools and interventions can make better public space tangible for City Changers (chapter 3)

What it takes to inspire as many people as possible to support your cause and actively get involved (chapter 4)

How various monitoring tools provide measurable arguments for redesign (chapter 5)

Finally, a step-by-step guide to turn an idea into a vision and a vision into change (chapter 6); a glossary of key terms follows the chapters

How this toolbox came to life

The Toolbox for City Changers is an Erasmus+-funded project that aims to give people the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence to shape change in their own neighbourhoods. Its three workshops brought together residents, local initiatives, and practitioners to share experiences and explore how to build a neighbourhood initiative, test ideas in public space, and engage in local decision-making. This handbook makes the tools and approaches available beyond the workshops, so that more people can adapt them to their own local context.

The three workshops

Superblocks & Playful Interventions, 5 March 2026 in Ljubljana: How local initiatives emerge, how superblocks work, and how playful actions attract attention.

Tactical Urbanism & Street Design, 24 April 2026 in Berlin: How temporary, low-cost interventions visibly open up different uses of street space, and how groups can develop street design visions together.

Organising & Monitoring Tools, 30 May 2026 online: How initiatives gain supporters, track progress, and structure their campaigns with goals, milestones, and shared tasks.
