# City Changers — practical brand guide

## Identity

The project name is Toolbox for City Changers. The mascot is The Green Fix.
Use the supplied logo instead of retyping or rebuilding its lettering.
The seven expressive static mascot poses are illustrations, not new official logos.
**Skeptical** preserves the original questioning, hand-at-chin pose. **Think** is
the smiling, raised-hand idea moment with yellow rays from the Think animation.

## Colours

Enter these HEX codes in your design app's colour picker.

```text

Paper             #FDFCF9   Pale background.
Dark background   #161511   Background for the Linen treatment.
Website green     #0C6B3C   Headings, accents and calls to action on light designs.
Mascot green      #176B43   Existing artwork colour; do not recolour the mascot.
Mascot ink        #20221C   Dark outlines and boots in the Ink treatment.
Linen             #CBBF9F   Pale outlines and boots in the Linen treatment.
Cream lettering   #F2EDE3   Full-logo lettering on dark backgrounds.
```

These are existing production colours, not a new palette. Keep the supplied
yellow and other mascot details unchanged. Website text uses #1D1A16; the mascot
outline uses #20221C. They are intentionally not the same token.
Use dark text on Paper and Cream on a dark background. Pale outlines are not
intended as small text colours. Test contrast when adding text over photos.
These screen colours are not a print/CMYK specification; ask a printer for proofs.

## Type

Fraunces: main headlines. Clarity City: supporting text, labels and short captions.
Newsreader: optional longer editorial passages, matching the handbook.
For an ordinary social post, stick to Fraunces and Clarity City. Use fewer words
and larger type rather than squeezing a paragraph into a small graphic.
The logo is supplied artwork: installing Fraunces does not recreate the wordmark.

## Layout

Keep the original proportions: resize from a corner with proportions locked.
Leave visible breathing room on every side. As a practical starting point, allow
at least one boot's height between the artwork and nearby text or the edge.
This is a working recommendation, not a newly approved measured logo standard.
Do not crop the leaf or boots, rotate the full logo, add effects, or recolour it.
Use the light/dark variant that stays clear against the whole background.
Place pointing captions to the right. Keep captions away from the mascot's face.
There is no fixed minimum social-logo size: check legibility at actual phone size.

## Writing and images

Write clear, welcoming, practical copy. Describe a specific community action and
give one next step. Verify claims and results before publishing. Do not invent
quotes, endorsements or campaign outcomes. Use real project photography only
after checking permission and credits; supporting images are not a stock library.
