TOOLBOX FOR CITY CHANGERS — START HERE

This is your ready-to-use design kit. No coding or software installation is
needed to use the pictures. Extract the ZIP before opening or importing files.
Keep this folder as your original copy; save your finished posts elsewhere.

YOUR FIRST POST

1. Open 00-previews/mascot-poses.png to choose a pose.
2. In your design app, create your post and add your photo or background.
3. Drag a PNG from 02-mascot-stills/ink onto a light background.
   For a dark background, choose the same pose from linen instead.
4. Add a short headline and the full logo from 01-logos if needed.
5. Check the post on a phone, then export from your design app as usual.

CHOOSING A LOGO

logo-lockup.svg       Full name and mascot, for light backgrounds.
logo-lockup-dark.svg  Full name and mascot, for dark backgrounds.
logo.svg             Mascot symbol, for light backgrounds.
logo-dark.svg        Mascot symbol, for dark backgrounds.
logo-lockup.png      Large transparent full logo, for light backgrounds.
logo.png             512px symbol on a pale square, NOT transparent.
logo-small.svg       Simplified symbol for tiny sizes, not the usual social logo.

Start with the full logo when introducing the project. SVG stays sharp at any
size; use PNG if your app does not accept SVG. For a transparent symbol PNG,
use 02-mascot-stills/ink/neutral.png. For a dark-background full logo, use its
SVG; there is no matching full-logo dark PNG in this kit.

CHOOSING A POSE

neutral / idle  General introductions; idle is the animated version.
wave            Welcome, hello or goodbye.
point           Draw attention to a caption on the mascot's right.
celebrate       Community wins and milestones.
think           Questions, ideas and planning.
step            Next steps and action; animated walking stays in place.

Ink means dark outlines, suited to light backgrounds. Linen means pale warm
outlines, suited to dark backgrounds. Both versions have transparent backgrounds:
they sit on top of your design without a square behind them. The colored
backgrounds in the preview sheets are only for comparison.

MAKING A VIDEO

The MOV files in 03-animation/video are transparent video overlays, not finished
Reels or Stories. Each is 1080 x 1080, one second long, at 30 frames per second.
They use ProRes 4444, a video format that preserves the transparent background.

1. Import a MOV into a video editor that supports ProRes transparency.
2. Put it on a layer ABOVE your footage or colored background.
3. Repeat the clip to keep the mascot moving for longer; these files have no sound.
4. Add your text and sound, then export the finished video as MP4 for posting.

Do not upload the overlay directly to Instagram expecting transparency to survive.
If your app rejects it or shows a black square, try a compatible editor, or use
a still PNG instead. MP4 cannot keep the overlay's transparent background.

CANVA AND LOTTIE

The JSON files in 03-animation/lottie contain vector animations, not pictures.
Use a Lottie-compatible app or Canva's LottieFiles integration, if available for
your account. Connect your workspace/import the JSON through that integration,
add it to your design, and preview the finished export. Availability and upload
options depend on the service and account; ordinary image upload may not work.
Your app controls repetition. Each file contains a one-second animation cycle.
If this is not supported, use a still PNG or the MOV workflow above.

FONTS

Use Fraunces for headlines and Clarity City for short captions. Newsreader is
included for longer handbook-style text. You do not need to install a font to
use the logos: their lettering is already drawn into the artwork.

To install fonts on a computer, open the TTF files and choose Install. Start with
FrauncesDisplay-Bold.ttf, ClarityCity-Regular.ttf and ClarityCity-Bold.ttf.
WOFF2 files are for websites; ignore them for everyday design work. Uploading
custom fonts to an online design app may require a paid plan or admin access.
Keep the OFL licence files if you pass the fonts to someone else.

BEFORE YOU POST

- Keep the mascot and logo in their original proportions and colors.
- Leave space around the leaf, boots and lettering. Do not place text over faces.
- Keep important text away from the edges and platform buttons; preview in-app.
- Use readable text, captions for speech, and useful alt text for still posts.
- Check spelling, links, photo permissions and required credits.
- The kit contains assets, not approved campaign copy or finished social templates.

RIGHTS AND PERMISSIONS

The inclusion of an image does not establish permission for every new use.
The supporting images are reference material from the handbook. Before publishing
them, confirm the photographer/creator credit, permission and any required
licence wording with the project team. Partner and Erasmus+ marks may have
separate usage rules; do not imply endorsement. A CC BY badge alone does not
tell you which files it applies to. Font permissions are in the included OFL
notices. The repository licence is included for reference, not as proof that
every photo or third-party mark is cleared. Trademark clearance is not included.

NEED SOMETHING CHANGED?

Ask the person who sent this kit for new exports, missing credits or a custom
animation. Editable animation source is deliberately not in this everyday kit.
Tell them the filename, background color and intended platform to save time.
