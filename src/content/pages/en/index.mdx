# Toolbox for City Changers

The "Toolbox for City Changers" is for people who want to make their neighbourhood safer, greener, more inclusive and more resilient to climate change, and need practical tools to do so.

## From idea to impact: a step-by-step guide to changing streets

This handbook guides you from your first idea to a finished street improvement. Start by exploring the [power of superblocks and playful interventions](book/02-the-power-of-superblocks.html) to make your vision visible. Use [tactical urbanism and street design tools](book/03-tactical-urbanism-and-street-design.html) to show how your streets could be different. Then [organise support](book/04-organising-and-strategies-for-change.html) and [monitor your impact](book/05-monitoring-making-impact-visible.html) to build evidence for your cause.

"Toolbox for City Changers" is a collaboration between [Wir machen Wien](https://wirmachen.wien), Berlin's [Changing Cities](https://changing-cities.org), and Ljubljana's [Prostorož](https://www.prostoroz.org).
