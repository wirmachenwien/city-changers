# Toolbox for City Changers

The "Toolbox for City Changers" is for people who want to make their neighbourhood safer, greener, more inclusive and more resilient to climate change, and need practical tools to do so.

<ButtonLink href={"book/01-why-this-toolbox.html"} label={"Open the handbook"} />

## This handbook guides your initiative from an idea for better streets to concrete change:

From a clear [concept of superblocks and playful interventions](book/02-the-power-of-superblocks.html) that make your vision visible, through [tactical urbanism and street design tools](book/03-tactical-urbanism-and-street-design.html) for a change of perspective in the neighbourhood, to [mobilisation](book/04-mobilisation-and-strategies-for-change.html) and [monitoring tools](book/05-monitoring-making-impact-visible.html) to gather evidence for your cause.

"Toolbox for City Changers" is a collaboration between [Wir machen Wien](https://wirmachen.wien), [Changing Cities](https://changing-cities.org) from Berlin and [Prostorož](https://www.prostoroz.org) from Ljubljana.

The content is by Fricka Lindemann, Zala Velkavrh, and Valentina Haas, the handbook design is by Bernhard Hayden.
